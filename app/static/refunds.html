Refund Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

We want you to be happy with BrickGains. This policy explains how refunds work for paid subscriptions operated by Mint Technology LLC.

1. Free plan

The Free plan costs nothing, so no refund is needed. You can use it for as long as you like.

2. 14-day money-back guarantee

If you are not satisfied with a Pro or Investor subscription, contact us within 14 days of your first payment and we will issue a full refund, no questions asked.

3. Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically each month. Charges for renewals are non-refundable, but you can cancel at any time to stop future charges; your plan stays active until the end of the current period.

4. How to request a refund or cancel

  • To cancel: open your account and cancel in one click, anytime.
  • To request a refund: email support@brickgains.com from your account email within the eligible window.

5. Contact

Any question about billing or refunds: support@brickgains.com.
