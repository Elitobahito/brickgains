Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how BrickGains, operated by Mint Technology LLC, uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We also use your browser's local storage for certain preferences.

2. Cookies we use

  • Essential, session: a bg_session cookie keeps you signed in. Without it, accounts would not work.
  • Preferences, local storage: we store your free-check counter and interface preferences in your browser. This data stays on your device.
  • Payments: when you subscribe, Stripe may set cookies needed to process payment securely.

We do not currently use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies will prevent you from staying logged in.

4. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. Questions? support@brickgains.com.
