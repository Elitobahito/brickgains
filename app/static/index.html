The reality

You have no idea what your LEGO really sells for.

And that's exactly how people end up selling for far too little.

Asking prices lie

BrickLink shows what sellers ask, not what buyers pay. Price by that and you scare buyers off, or leave money on the table.

Forecasts are just bets

Buyers bank on your doubt

FAQ

Good questions

Where do the prices come from?

Live listings from the BrickLink marketplace (the world's biggest LEGO secondary market), plus official retail prices and retirement dates. Updated regularly.

Is it accurate?

We show real market prices (new and used, min, average and max), not a black-box estimate. You always see the range.

How do "sell now" alerts work?

When a set you own retires or hits a new price high, we ping you. That's usually the best window to sell.

Can I import my whole collection?

Yes. Bulk CSV import is available on the Investor plan, with multi-copy tracking for resellers.
