Billing FAQ

Questions about plans

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever, no card required. You get 3 value checks per day and can track up to 10 sets.

Can I cancel anytime?

Anytime, in one click from your account. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period, no questions asked.

What do I get by upgrading to Pro?

Unlimited value checks, your full portfolio dashboard with ROI, Market Movers, watchlist buy signals, the eBay profit calculator, bulk import and a shareable portfolio. Email price alerts and price-history charts are included too.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major cards via Stripe, our secure payment processor. Your card details never touch our servers.

Do you offer refunds?

BrickGains is a digital product, so payments are non-refundable. You can try everything on the Free plan first, and cancel your subscription anytime in one click to stop any future charge.
