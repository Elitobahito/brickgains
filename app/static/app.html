📷 Scan a box

Use your phone camera to scan a LEGO box barcode and add the set to your portfolio in one tap — no typing.

1Open the camera

Tap the button below and allow camera access when your browser asks.

2Point at the barcode

Frame the barcode printed on the LEGO box — we read it automatically, no need to press anything.

3Added for you

We identify the set and drop it into your portfolio. Just add the price you paid and you're done.

📱 Works best on Android Chrome. On iPhone, Safari doesn't support live camera scanning yet — tap the button and you'll be able to type the set number instead (it's printed right next to the barcode).
